# Wiki typography

The wiki self-hosts the Latin WOFF2 subsets of [Manrope](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Manrope) and [Instrument Serif](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Instrument+Serif), served by Google Fonts. No font network requests are made at runtime.

Both are distributed under the SIL Open Font License. Their notices are included as `manrope-OFL.txt` and `instrument-serif-OFL.txt`. The font binaries retain their original family names.
