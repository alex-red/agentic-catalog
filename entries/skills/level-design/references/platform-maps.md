# Platform maps and farming loops

A repeatable farming map is an interaction among controller capability, attack coverage, terrain, spawns and rewards. Its quality cannot be inferred from a platform diagram alone.

## Compare routes

Sketch plausible loops through spawn areas and return paths. For each supported build, consider traversal time, attack time, enemy return cadence, pickup cost, required attention and recovery after an error. A stationary strategy can be intentional; a moving route can be busy without creating worthwhile decisions.

Identify capability breakpoints: an upgrade might allow a platform to be reached, a group to be defeated in one action or two spawn areas to be covered together. Compare the route before and after the breakpoint. Increasing theoretical damage without changing actual clearing behavior may provide little perceptible improvement.

Inspect camera framing and off-screen activity. Players should have enough information to choose movement or defense; a memorized spawn cycle is a different skill from reacting to a visible threat. Make the intended source of mastery explicit.

## Check shared space

Consider what happens when another player joins. Are enemies or rewards depleted? Can players divide platforms, contribute complementary coverage or receive shared credit? Does a high-mobility build displace everyone else? Channel count and instancing affect encounter frequency as well as capacity.

Reward efficiency can make one route dominate even when alternatives are mechanically viable. Compare actual experience, currency, item access and group rewards per relevant session budget. Do not infer a healthy choice from equal enemy counts.

## Useful experiments

- Compare two loops using the same controller, attack kit and spawn rules. Observe error recovery and effort alongside yield.
- Test a new capability on an existing route before adding new terrain. Check whether it creates an option or invalidates previous space.
- Add a second player under explicit reward rules and observe whether cooperation is welcome, irrelevant or harmful.

Use the target game's real constants when available. Any proposed spawn rate, reward multiplier or traversal margin is a tuning assumption until tested. Agent route execution can reveal mechanical problems; sustained comfort, social outcomes and enjoyment require appropriate player evidence.
