# Game balance review

Use for a concrete combat, economy, progression or loot system with rules or data to inspect. Produce evidence-backed findings and bounded tuning proposals, not a claim that design intent or a spreadsheet proves enjoyable play.

## Establish the comparison

Identify the target build, domain, player cohorts, progression stages, supported strategies and intended experience. Read the relevant rules, formulas, data and stated targets using the project's existing conventions. Keep a short source list and distinguish intended values from implemented values and measured outcomes.

If inputs are missing, name what can and cannot be assessed. A rule can support a structural finding without telemetry, but absent data cannot support measured acquisition time or player prevalence. If all consequential inputs are absent, return a preparation plan or request the decisive input; do not issue a healthy verdict.

Missing target ranges do not prevent investigating mathematical contradictions, unreachable gates or reproducible resource-generation exploits. Conversely, a value within its documented range does not establish that the range is appropriate.

## Analyze the relevant domain

### Combat and strategy

Compare options under explicit assumptions: encounter length, setup, range, accuracy, cooldowns, resource costs, target defenses, mobility and utility. Raw damage per second alone can misrepresent burst, survivability and team contribution. Distinguish strict dominance under comparable conditions from high popularity or a situational advantage.

Check failure and defense interactions, including invulnerability loops, unavoidable damage and scaling differences between early and late progression. A powerful strategy is a finding only in relation to the intended challenge or a broken contract; asymmetry is not automatically a defect.

### Resources and markets

Track currency and item sources, transfers, conversion and permanent removal separately. Trading redistributes; taxes remove currency; item destruction affects item supply. Inspect how rates depend on player population, character count, automation, progression and time.

Examine accumulation, replacement demand, exchange bottlenecks and newcomer access. A loop without a sink is not inherently critical: distinguish intended abundance, a closed match, and persistent runaway accumulation. Do not equate aggregate currency growth with every item's price inflation.

### Progression and gates

Compare requirements with the actual reward and power trajectory. Experience-to-next-level alone does not give time-to-level; reward rates and access change too. Inspect dead zones, capability breakpoints, catch-up paths and the ability of friends at different stages to participate together.

Trace prerequisites and alternate paths for unreachable or circular gates. Separate intended long-term goals from accidentally stalled progress, and inspect the burden of recurring tasks across unlocked systems rather than one task in isolation.

### Loot and uncertain upgrades

State the probability model, attempt duration, costs, eligibility and recovery assumptions. Check dependencies, pity, replacement items, trade access, regression and event modifiers before treating attempts as independent.

Compare median and adverse-tail cost/time as well as expectation. For constant independent success probability p greater than zero, expected attempts are 1/p, but this does not describe a guaranteed budget or a model with changing probabilities. Distinguish analytic estimates, simulated distributions and observed player outcomes. Do not invent precision from unknown rates.

## Assess and propose

For each consequential finding, record the evidence, affected conditions, consequence, uncertainty and a targeted change. Judge severity by actual impact and recovery, not a universal verdict tied to one formula or missing sink. Keep unassessed areas visible; do not blend them into a clean bill of health.

Prefer a change that tests the suspected cause. Consider adjacent systems, player segments and existing investments before retuning. Preserve the user's authority over intended balance; do not replace an unconventional but intentional design with a generic industry default.

For authorized changes, verify calculations and affected data contracts, then test representative scenarios and the relevant extremes. For a review-only request, stop at findings and proposed experiments. Report what was inspected, what was calculated or executed, what remains hypothetical and what player observation could overturn the recommendation.
