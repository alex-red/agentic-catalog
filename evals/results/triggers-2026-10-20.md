## Conclusions

- The skill descriptions were not the problem. On a fresh request, every skill fired with both the old and the new wording, both in the sandbox and in the user's real environment.
- The description rewrite produced no measurable trigger gain. It did fix one frontmatter block that was not valid YAML.
- The open question is mid-session: whether agents check visible changes after making them. A single-request trigger check cannot measure that. It needs an outcome evaluation with a UI change and a running app, which remains to be built.
